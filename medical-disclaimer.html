When to Seek Emergency Care

Some symptoms require immediate medical attention. If you experience any of the following, do not wait to schedule an appointment — seek emergency care right away.

⚠️ Call 911 or Go to the Nearest Emergency Room

  • Sudden, severe back or neck pain after a fall, motor vehicle accident, or other trauma
  • Loss of bladder or bowel control (new urinary retention, incontinence, or inability to control bowels)
  • Progressive weakness in the arms or legs
  • Numbness in the saddle area — the groin, inner thighs, or buttocks
  • Fever above 101°F combined with back pain
  • Sudden, severe headache — the worst headache of your life
  • Signs of stroke — remember FAST: Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call 911
  • Chest pain or shortness of breath
  • Any condition you believe requires immediate medical attention

Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. Do not wait to schedule an appointment.
