## Notes

- All URLs verified live April 23, 2026 via WebFetch / WebSearch
- MedlinePlus URLs are extremely stable (US government, NIH-maintained)
- Cleveland Clinic URLs follow `/health/diseases/{number}-{slug}/` pattern that's been stable for years
- NIH/NINDS/NIAMS URLs are very stable
- Use these as inline `<a href="...">` links in the body content
- Wrap the specific claim with the link, e.g., "[85-90% of patients improve](URL)"
- Add `rel="noopener"` for external links (no `nofollow` — these are authoritative citations, not paid links)
