Local map rankings: a note on volatility

The local map rankings (the "3-pack" you see at the top of Google Maps search results) have been volatile in May. This is normal — whenever a practice ships major changes to their site or Google profile, Google's algorithm takes 4–8 weeks to re-evaluate and settle. We're in the middle of that window.

Two map keywords showed sustained improvement across the full month:

  • "Pain management doctor near me" — average rank improved from 8.4 to 7.6, and the practice appeared in the local 3-pack on 2 different grid points across Katy for the first time
  • "Pain specialist near me" — average rank improved 2.5 positions (12.6 to 10.1), with top-10 coverage nearly doubling (8 grid points to 15)

One keyword — "epidural steroid injection katy" — briefly hit the local 3-pack on 5 grid points in early May before correcting back to its baseline. This kind of week-to-week swing during the recovery window is expected and not a concern. The conversion-tier metrics (calls, clicks, website traffic) are the durable signal that the work is delivering.
