# AI Overview Baseline Analysis — June 3, 2026

Based on 15 query screenshots in `screenshots/`. Captured by Rameel in Chrome incognito, Katy TX location.

## Summary findings

- **13 of 15 queries trigger an AI Overview** (Q8 and Q15 do not)
- **drimranqureshi.com is cited on 0 of 15** — the expected baseline
- **The cited sources are overwhelmingly major institutions:** Cleveland Clinic (11 of 13 AIO queries), Johns Hopkins (7), Mayo Clinic (4), HSS (3), plus NIH/PMC, FDA, AAOS, WebMD, NHS, Duke, UCLA, NIAMS

## The strategic reality

**Pure informational medical AIO is dominated by large health systems and .gov/.edu sources.** For YMYL (your-money-your-life) medical queries, Google's AI Overview heavily favors institutional authority. A single physician practice will essentially never displace Cleveland Clinic for "what is an epidural steroid injection."

**Conclusion: chasing AIO citation on generic informational queries is a low-probability play.** We should NOT spend Month 4 trying to get cited for "what is PRP therapy" — Cleveland Clinic owns that and will keep owning it.

## Where we CAN win (the pivot)

### Play 1: Localized medical queries (highest-value, most winnable)

Institutions don't build city-specific pages. "Peripheral neuropathy treatment" cites Mayo/Cleveland Clinic — but **"peripheral neuropathy treatment in katy"** is a different SERP where the local provider wins. Dr. Q already gets impressions for these localized queries (per May GSC):

- `peripheral neuropathy treatment in katy` (18–21 impressions)
- `knee pain relief in katy` (15–16)
- `shoulder pain treatment in katy` (15–18)
- `radiofrequency and ultrasound treatments katy, tx area` (16–18)
- `regenerative therapy katy tx` (60 impressions — highest)
- `back pain fulshear`, `pain management doctors sugar land`, etc.

**Action:** These are organic + local-pack plays, not generic-AIO plays. Make sure every one of these has a dedicated, well-structured page section that answers the query directly. Several already exist (condition pages, city pages); the work is making them rank #1 locally.

### Play 2: Queries that don't trigger AIO at all (pure organic #1)

- **Q8 "is PRP effective for knee arthritis"** — no AIO. Organic results: Mayo, NIH/PMC, Johns Hopkins. We have `/blog/prp-vs-cortisone-injections` and `/treatments/prp-therapy`. Goal: rank these on page 1. No AIO to chase.
- **Q15 "cervical epidural injection what to expect"** — no AIO. Organic: Cleveland Clinic, Kaiser, UMass. We have `/treatments/cervical-epidural-injection` and `/blog/epidural-steroid-injection-what-to-expect`. Goal: rank organically.

**Action:** These are won by ranking, not citation. Treat as standard organic SEO targets.

### Play 3: The one generic query with beatable sources

**Q12 "when should I see a pain management doctor"** — the AIO cited generic orthopedic/pain-clinic sources (NOT the big institutions). We have `/blog/when-to-see-a-pain-specialist`. This is the ONE generic informational query where AIO citation is realistic, because the incumbent sources are weak.

**Action:** Optimize `/blog/when-to-see-a-pain-specialist` aggressively for AIO:
- Answer-first block: direct 2-sentence answer to "when should I see a pain management doctor" in the first 100 words
- FAQPage schema with the exact question
- Clear bulleted "see a pain doctor if..." list (AIO loves extractable lists)
- Strong external citations (the page already has E-E-A-T treatment)

## What this means for the Month 4 AIO work

**Revise the Month 4 AIO plan.** The original plan assumed we'd chase AIO citation on the 5 highest-impression queries. The baseline shows that's mostly futile for generic medical queries. Instead:

1. **Drop generic-informational AIO chasing** (Q1–7, Q9–11, Q13–14 in their generic form). Cleveland Clinic owns these.
2. **Double down on localized query optimization** (Play 1) — the real, winnable opportunity. This overlaps with our existing local SEO work.
3. **Rank the no-AIO queries organically** (Play 2) — standard SEO.
4. **Make ONE focused AIO play on Q12** (Play 3) — the only realistic generic-AIO target.
5. **Add AEO/Bing work** (already in Month 4 plan) — ChatGPT/Perplexity may cite local providers more readily than Google AIO does, since their grounding is different.
