## Notes on GBP post best practices (from the playbook)

- Keep posts under 1,500 characters (Google truncates at ~1,500)
- Always include a CTA button (Call, Book, Learn More, Visit)
- Use 1 emoji at the start as a visual anchor — Google doesn't reward emoji stuffing
- Photos > no photos every time
- Don't repeat the same post within 90 days (Google flags it as spam)
- Health-related posts are scrutinized — keep claims modest, no "cures" or "guaranteed results"
