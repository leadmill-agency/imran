## Why Rankings Haven't Moved (Honest Answer)

**The short version:**

> SEO is a 3-6 month game. Maps rankings specifically depend on GBP completion + reviews + citations — none of which we've built yet. Those are Month 3 priorities.

**The longer version:**

1. **Organic rankings lag content by 3-6 months.** Google needs to crawl, index, evaluate, and trust new content before it ranks. We built our major content in Month 2. Movement is expected in Month 4-5.

2. **Maps rankings are a completely different algorithm** than organic. Google Maps weighs:
   - **GBP completeness** (categories, services, photos, Q&A, description) — we haven't completed this yet
   - **Review volume + velocity + keywords** — competitors have 3x our volume
   - **Citations** (consistent NAP across directories) — we haven't built these yet
   - **Proximity to searcher** (we can't control)
   - **Domain age + trust** (time-based)

3. **Competitors have 4-7 years of accumulated trust.** Silky Patel since 2018. Katy Pain Specialists since 2015+. That's not something we can short-circuit.

4. **What we built in Months 1-2 is the foundation.** The content, schema, site architecture, internal linking — all of it supports Months 3-6 ranking growth. Without this foundation, no amount of citations or reviews would rank.
