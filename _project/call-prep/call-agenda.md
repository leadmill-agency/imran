# Call Agenda & Talking Points — Dr. Imran Qureshi

## Goal of the Call

1. Demonstrate value delivered in Months 1-2
2. Reset expectations on SEO/Maps timeline (honestly, not defensively)
3. Show a concrete path to close the gap with Silky Patel
4. Get buy-in for revised Month 3 scope (GLP-1 + PRP emphasis)
5. Leave him feeling like you're a partner, not a vendor

## Duration: ~45 minutes

---

## Agenda

### 1. Opening & Acknowledgment (2-3 min)

**Say:**
> "Thanks for setting this up. I appreciate the direct feedback in your email — it's actually really helpful because it tells me exactly what you're paying attention to. Before I walk through progress, I want to acknowledge upfront: you're right that rankings haven't moved meaningfully yet. That's expected at this point in the timeline, and I want to walk you through why — and more importantly, what we're doing about it in Month 3."

**Key framing:**
- Don't be defensive. Own the flat data.
- Show you understand what he actually cares about: new patients, not website pretty-ness.
- Set the meeting as a working session, not a status update.

---

### 2. Timeline Clarification (2 min)

**Say:**
> "Quick housekeeping: I know you mentioned 3 months, but checking the invoices — first paid Feb 20, so we're actually at about 8 weeks of active work, not 12. I'm mentioning this because SEO timelines are really sensitive at the month-2 vs month-3 mark. At 2 months, flat rankings are expected. By month 4-5 is when we should start seeing movement."

**If he pushes back:** Acknowledge his reality matters more than the invoice date. Move on.

---

### 3. What's Been Built (8-10 min)

Walk through the progress report. Hit these highlights:

- **Site growth**: 32 pages → 44 pages
- **7 new content pages** in Month 2 (3 procedure pages, 2 hub pages, 2 blog posts)
- **Full schema coverage** (procedure, condition, physician, FAQ, business, article)
- **Local SEO foundation laid**: MedicalBusiness schema on 21 pages that didn't have it
- **Technical SEO audit**: 9 issues found and fixed
- **Client feedback applied**: ESI page, MRI claims corrected, experience updated, new reviews added

**Say:**
> "This is the foundation. Nothing we do in Month 3 onward — citations, reviews, GBP optimization — would work without this infrastructure being in place. That's why we prioritized it."

---

### 4. Why Rankings Haven't Moved — The Honest Answer (5 min)

This is the most important part of the call. Don't rush it.

**Say (revised):**
> "There's a real answer to your question about rankings, and I want to give it to you straight.
>
> Google Maps rankings are driven by a completely separate algorithm from organic search. The signals are: GBP completeness, review volume and velocity, citations across directories, and domain age. Good news — you've already been working on GBP completeness (I can see CLS Health is now linked, you're posting weekly, photos are up). That's a big lift and it matters.
>
> What hasn't been done yet:
> - **Citations**: 25+ medical and general directories with consistent NAP — this is the biggest remaining off-site lever, and it's on my side to execute
> - **Review acceleration**: system to capture reviews systematically from every patient visit — both our side (tools) and yours (front desk workflow)
> - **Profile age**: time-based, can't force — Silky has 7 years, you have 1. We close this gap by compounding everything else faster
>
> By month 4-5, you should see Maps movement from citations propagating. Month 6+ is when review velocity + profile maturity start compounding."

**Expected pushback:**
- "Why didn't we do GBP optimization first?"
  → *Because GBP optimization without a corresponding, keyword-rich website underneath it doesn't convert. Google also looks at whether the landing destination (your site) matches the GBP claims. Now that the site is solid, GBP work amplifies it.*

## Things NOT to Do on This Call

- ❌ Don't get defensive about flat rankings — own it and pivot to what's changing
- ❌ Don't overpromise a specific timeline (e.g., "you'll rank #1 by July") — ranges only
- ❌ Don't blame external factors (Google algorithm changes, competitors) without acknowledging what's in your control
- ❌ Don't push scope expansion beyond what he's already asked for — GLP-1 and PRP are plenty
- ❌ Don't mention paid ads unprompted — wait to see if he brings it up or save for Month 4

## Things TO Do

- ✅ Use his language ("you already beat her on rating")
- ✅ Show the data side-by-side (don't just describe it)
- ✅ Give him concrete action items he can execute immediately
- ✅ Set a specific next check-in date
- ✅ Send recap email within 2 hours of call ending
