## 2. Review Acceleration Strategy

**Current: 52 reviews | Target: 100+ reviews by month 6**

**Immediate actions:**
- [ ] Create a Google review short link (search "Google review link generator" for your business)
- [ ] Print QR code for the review link — post at front desk, in treatment rooms, on business cards
- [ ] Add review request to post-visit email/text workflow (check if your EHR supports this)

**Front desk script:**
> "Thank you for coming in today! If you had a good experience, we'd really appreciate it if you could leave us a Google review — it helps other patients in the Katy area find us. I can text you the link if that's easier."

**Review response protocol:**
- Respond to EVERY review within 24-48 hours
- Positive reviews: Thank the patient, mention the treatment or condition naturally ("We're glad the epidural injection provided relief for your sciatica")
- Negative reviews: Acknowledge, apologize, offer to resolve offline ("Please call our office at 281-982-2144 so we can address this directly")

**Keyword-rich reviews (organic, not coached):**
Patients who naturally mention their treatment or condition in reviews help local rankings. Example: "Dr. Qureshi gave me an epidural injection for my herniated disc and I feel so much better." Don't ask patients to say specific words — just make it easy for them to leave a review soon after treatment when the experience is fresh.
