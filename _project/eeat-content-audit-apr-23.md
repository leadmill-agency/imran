## Top 5 Changes (impact-per-effort, ranked)

### 1. Add "Medically reviewed by" byline to every condition + treatment page
**Impact: HIGH | Effort: 2/10 | Estimated lift: +15-25% on symptom queries**

Add visible byline near top:
> Medically reviewed by Dr. Imran Qureshi, D.O.
> Board-Certified Interventional Spine & Sports Medicine Physician
> Last reviewed: [Month YYYY]

Affects: 9 condition pages + 9 treatment pages = 18 pages

### 2. Link to external medical authorities for major claims
**Impact: HIGH | Effort: 3/10 | Estimated lift: +10-20% on informational queries**

Currently 80% of pages cite zero external sources. Add 3-5 authoritative links per page to:
- Mayo Clinic (`mayoclinic.org`)
- NIH / MedlinePlus (`medlineplus.gov`)
- Cleveland Clinic (`my.clevelandclinic.org`)
- Johns Hopkins (`hopkinsmedicine.org`)

Example: Replace "Studies show 85-90% of patients improve without surgery" with "[Studies show](https://www.mayoclinic.org/...) 85-90% of patients improve without surgery."

### 3. Create standalone `/medical-disclaimer` page
**Impact: MEDIUM | Effort: 2/10 | Estimated lift: +5-10%**

Single new page with:
- Full medical disclaimer
- Author credentials (Dr. Qureshi)
- Editorial review process
- When to seek emergency care
- Last updated date

Link from every footer. This is a standard YMYL trust signal.

### 4. Expand `/about` with detailed credentials
**Impact: MEDIUM | Effort: 2/10 | Estimated lift: +3-8%**

Add:
- "Education & Credentials" section (med school, residency, fellowship names with years)
- "Board Certifications" prominently displayed (Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation)
- "Professional Memberships" (societies, associations)
- "Areas of Expertise" detailed list

### 5. Restructure condition page openings with 75-125 word answer block
**Impact: MEDIUM | Effort: 4/10 | Estimated lift: +8-15% on question queries**

Current condition pages open with clinical context. Replace first paragraph with direct answer block targeting the implied patient question.

Example for `/low-back-pain`:

**Before:**
> "Low back pain affects up to 80% of adults in their lifetime..."

**After:**
> "Most low back pain improves with non-surgical treatment, especially when caused by muscle strain, mild disc bulges, or facet joint inflammation. Pain that includes leg weakness, loss of bladder or bowel control, or worsening numbness should be evaluated immediately. At our Katy, TX office, Dr. Imran Qureshi diagnoses the specific source of your pain through physical exam and imaging, then matches treatment to the cause — typically epidural steroid injections, nerve blocks, or radiofrequency ablation."

## H2 Heading Pattern Issue

Current H2s on most pages are clinical labels:
- "Conditions Treated"
- "Treatment Options"
- "How the Procedure Works"
- "Recovery and Results"

Playbook recommends question-based patient H2s:
- "What conditions does this treat?"
- "What treatments help?"
- "How does the procedure work?"
- "What does recovery look like?"

This is a stylistic shift more than rewriting — affects 18+ pages.

---

## Source Citation Gaps (Examples)

Pages currently make claims without citations:
- "85-90% of low back pain improves without surgery" — needs Mayo Clinic / NIH source
- "80-90% of herniated discs improve nonsurgically" — needs source
- "Most ESIs provide relief for 2-6 months" — needs clinical guidelines source
- "Radiofrequency ablation provides 6-18 months of relief" — needs source

Estimated 30-50 unsourced claims across 18 main pages. Adding 1-2 citations per claim = ~50-100 outbound links to add.
